## Primary shipped gameplay promise (source contract)

Rare Overworld `better_buried_encounters:buried_encounter` surface structures advertise a half-buried vanilla TNT pile and buried `better_buried_encounters:sealed_monster` blocks. The structure set uses random spread (`spacing: 48`, `separation: 16`, `salt: 163840271`) and an Overworld biome tag. `BuriedEncounterPiece` places an 8-block TNT pile (2×2×2, subject to valid terrain and bounding-box placement), deterministic surface clues and up to 32 sealed blocks from `FieldLayout.create`; it skips placements when height/bounds or block writes prevent them. `FieldProfile` selects radius 16 normally or 32 when `explosionoverhaul` is loaded. The monster roster prefers available registered hostile types from Born in Chaos / Alex's Caves and vanilla, falling back to vanilla zombie or silverfish. Removing a sealed block server-side releases its stored mob; an explosion tracker watches for optional direct chunk removal and releases it on the next scan, avoiding a second release via the normal removal callback. These are source intentions, **not** measured structure frequency, 32 guaranteed placed blocks, safety of exploding a field near a base, or proof of a generated field in the selected pack.

Sources: `src/main/resources/data/better_buried_encounters/worldgen/{structure/buried_encounter.json,structure_set/better_buried_encounters.json}`, `src/main/resources/data/better_buried_encounters/tags/worldgen/biome/has_structure/buried_encounter.json`, `worldgen/{BuriedEncounterStructure,BuriedEncounterPiece,FieldLayout,FieldProfile,MonsterRoster,ExplosionRemovalTracker,MonsterSpawner}.java`, `block/SealedMonsterBlock.java`, `blockentity/SealedMonsterBlockEntity.java`. The saved piece encodes center and explosion-overhaul choice (`cx/cy/cz/eo`); world continuity must be evaluated before changing structure placement, registry IDs or serialized data.
